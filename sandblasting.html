Our signature service

Buildings that looked beyond saving, brought back like new

Sandblasting is the fastest, most complete way to strip masonry paint, tar, soot and weathering from brick and stone. Customers tell us the same thing again and again: they couldn't believe it was the same building.

We blast exteriors, interiors, beams, fireplaces, steelwork, machinery and even boat hulls — with the latest, most powerful equipment, protection for your windows and doors, and a full tidy-up afterwards.

  • House fronts, gable ends and full exteriors
  • Internal brick, stone, beams and fireplaces
  • Steelwork, plant, machinery and boat hulls
  • Repointing, silicone and waterproofing arranged
