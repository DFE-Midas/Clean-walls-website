The right choice for period and listed stonework

Not every building should be blasted. Where stone is soft, carved or protected, our chemical cleaning process lifts soot, pollution and biological staining without abrading the surface — we've used it on Grade II listed buildings, Victorian terraces and Rochdale's own library.

We'll always tell you honestly which method your building needs; plenty of our five-star reviews mention us recommending the cheaper option.
